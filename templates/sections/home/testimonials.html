The card: a reel of tiles on top and the quote below, side by side from lg (at md the quote column beside the reel was only about 225px wide). Below sm the card reaches 1rem into the section's side padding (-mx-4), the same width as the course cards on phones (343px at 375px wide). Prev/next or the arrow keys (with the card focused) move one testimonial. The middle column holds the portraits: 3 plain tiles, then each portrait with 2 plain tiles between portraits, then 3 more. The outer columns are plain tiles in the same number, so they always cover the frame however many testimonials there are. (The original gave them fewer, and they ran out past about four.) Colours moved to the palette: the white card sits on the ice-blue section, the tiles fade ice-blue to white. The portraits show in full colour: the original's greyscale layer and purple sheen over them were removed by the user's choice, so the photos stay clear. The reel is aria-hidden: the name under each quote already says who it is, and three portraits read out at once would be noise, so the photos have no alt text. Quote text is deep-navy on white, about 14.8:1; the optional role under the name is deep-navy at 70%, about 7.5:1.
