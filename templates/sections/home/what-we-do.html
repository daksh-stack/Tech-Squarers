{% comment %} "What we do" — the three pillars from the company mission, as cards pinned to a ruled page and joined by a moving dashed line. Ported from a React component (how-it-works.tsx) to plain Django, Tailwind and CSS; the card itself is components/pinned-card.html. The copy lives here on purpose, not in admin — the user's call, as with the hero: it is fixed marketing copy, taken word for word from the company mission (the promise line and the three pillar descriptions). The heading and promise line are the section's existing write-up, kept above the component. Layout — a grid at every size: - phones: one column, cards stacked and centred - from md: every card spans two rows, each starting one row after the last — Training rows 1-2, Mentoring rows 2-3, Building rows 3-4. Sharing a row is what staggers them. auto-rows-fr makes every row the same height, so the step from 01 to 02 is exactly the step from 02 to 03. And Training and Building never share a row, so they can never overlap however tall the text makes them. - md: two equal columns, cards centred in them - from lg: one card per row, in rows of 330px | 330px | auto, so each card starts exactly 330px below the last — the spacing the user set. Columns of 15% | 280px | the rest | 280px | 15% put the cards where the React original had them horizontally: 280px wide, 15% in from each edge. Tilt is 2° on phones and 4° from md (the original's 8° was cut by request), alternating. The dashed line is drawn by static/js/pinned-path.js from wherever the cards actually are and redrawn whenever the layout resizes: straight segments from card centre to card centre, which on phones is a short straight drop between stacked cards. With no JavaScript it is simply empty; the cards are unaffected. .pinned-path (input.css) keeps the dashes flowing on both lines — continuous, like the hero typewriter, so it misses WCAG 2.2.2 (Pause, Stop, Hide); reduced motion stops it. Background: faint ruled lines every 32px, faded out toward both edges by white gradients so they sit only behind the middle of the section, as in the original. Deep-navy rather than the original's hardcoded black, since templates don't carry hex values. Mentoring takes the dark panel and the other two the light one, keeping the original's alternating colour within the brand palette. overflow-x-clip stops a tilted card's corner from pushing the page sideways. Scrolling in, the heading and promise line blur-rise into view (static/js/reveal.js). The cards and the dashed line don't: the line reached the cards before they had risen in, which looked broken, so the user had the diagram simply show. {% endcomment %}
