{% comment %} Pill button with a white circle at its right end. On hover the circle expands to fill the pill, a second copy of the label is revealed in vibrant blue behind the same expanding clip, and the arrow swaps: the resting one slides right and shrinks while a new one slides in from the left. Ported from the 21st.dev arrow-fill-button (React + lucide). `isolate` matters: the layers below are stacked with z-10 to z-40, and a plain `relative` root does not contain them. Without it they land in the page's root stacking context, where z-40 ties with the sticky navbar and wins on document order, so the white arrow circle painted over the bar on scroll. The original sizes everything in vw for a full-screen hero. Here the sizes are fixed, so the button keeps its shape inside the navbar.
